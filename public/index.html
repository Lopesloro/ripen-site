01

Every coin launched here pays its holders

Creator fees from each trade collect in one payout wallet. Every five minutes they are split between everyone holding at least 0.05% of the supply, and sent in $RIPE.

02

Your share ripens every hour

A wallet that just bought counts 1x. After a day it counts 2.7x, after a week 8.8x. The same bag earns more each day it stays put.

03

Fully ripe at three weeks

The curve climbs fastest in the first days and reaches 12x at day 21, where it stays for as long as you hold.

04

Selling picks it early

Sell half the bag and half the hold time leaves with it. Sell everything and you start from zero. Buying more mixes fresh coins in, so hold time is averaged.

05

The formula

Each split goes by weight. Weights are sampled every 30 seconds, so a wallet that bought a minute before a split earns about a minute's worth.

weight = coins × seconds held × ripeness
ripeness = 1x at buy → 12x at 21 days
